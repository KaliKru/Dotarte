FAQ

What would you hire me for?

For processes that have grown complicated and need to become simple again. I’m a product designer with 6 years of experience in the HR space: internal tools, websites and platforms. My background in visual design makes sure a solution not only works, but also looks clear.

How do I approach problems?

Understand first, then design. Since 2014, I’ve listened to users before making decisions. Back then it was packaging tests behind a one-way mirror, today it’s interviews, process analysis and usability tests. Often the real problem lies somewhere other than where you first look.

How do I work with others?

Closely and early. I talk to developers during the concept phase, not just at handoff. My basic knowledge of HTML, CSS and JavaScript helps with that. The people around the product matter just as much to me. For years, I coordinated daily with a key client, interviewed stakeholders and reported to management. For me, good collaboration comes from regular contact and from following through on what we agreed.

What impact have I had?
  • Internal tool, end-to-end from research to rollout: 50+ active users, approx. 20 % less recruiting effort
  • Application flow: from 5 steps to 2, approx. 30 % fewer drop-offs
  • Complaint form: approx. 50 % faster response time
  • Websites: Even when nobody knew exactly what was needed at the start, the result was sites that have been in use for years and are easy to keep developing.
What drives me?

The question of why something works or doesn’t. That’s why I keep learning, even after years in practice. In 2026, I deepened my product design and UX skills in two courses, and before that I learned JavaScript to better understand development. What I enjoy most is when a process everyone used to be annoyed by suddenly goes unnoticed.

Do I use AI?

Yes, as a tool. I use it for research, structuring and quick prototypes, for example with ChatGPT, Claude and Figma Make. I also built this website with AI: the design, content and decisions are mine, and the AI wrote the code following my direction. The responsibility for the result stays with me.

What am I looking for?

A permanent position as a product designer, in Berlin or remote. I prefer working with a team long term.
